# sim/io-cell-study

Study of the PDK's own `sg13g2_io` pad cells as the AIB pin (issue #196, operator ruling q107,
2026-11-18: "study the PDK I/O cells at 80 um pitch first, adopt the route if it delivers on the
spec"). Pin capacitance (T-24), driver output resistance (T-08) and a receiver level check, over
PVT-FULL, on the cells exactly as the PDK ships them. **Simulated** (extracted-capacitance
estimate from model small-signal admittance; no bond pad, no PEX, no layout).

**Status: one record, `records/20261118T213945Z/` (45/45 corners solved, batch fleet, runner
klt 0.9.0). T-16 over the channel with this cell is NOT measured** (see "What is not shown").
The study therefore does not meet the ruling's adoption test, and **no switch is proposed**: the
numbers go back to the operator (issue #196) beside option (a).

## What was run

`request.json` (generated by `gen_io_cell.py`, run by `run_io_cell.sh`) on `tb_io_cell.cir`: one
`klt sim` request, 5 MOS corners x VDDIO 1.08/1.20/1.32 V x -40/27/125 C = 45 units, each
process bundle carrying the thick-oxide `cornerMOShv.lib` section as well as the thin-oxide one
(the cells use `sg13_hv_*` devices). The PDK netlist `libs.ref/sg13g2_io/spice/sg13g2_io.spi`
is included by `$PDK_ROOT` path and instantiated unmodified: `IOPadInOut4mA`, `16mA`, `30mA`
(driver tristated for capacitance) and `IOPadIn` (receive only). Nothing is graded (no
`limits`): the record holds ranges, graded here against the spec.

The cell has its own `iovdd`, which the AIB block does not otherwise have. Two modes, both in
the record (`modes` field):

- **p**: `iovdd = 2.75 x VDDIO` (the PDK-characterised point: Liberty corners are 1.2 V core /
  3.3 V iovdd, 1.08 / 3.0, 1.32 / 3.6; here 2.97-3.63 V).
- **a**: `iovdd = VDDIO`, the AIB 1.2 V signalling level. The PDK does not characterise this.

One bench fix worth carrying: the PDK subcircuits tie the poly-resistor bulk to a node named
`sub!`, local to each subcircuit unless declared `.global`; left floating the matrix is singular
and the operating point never converges (the first attempt timed out on it).

## Results (worst case over the 45 corners)

**T-24, pin capacitance, fF** (T-24: target <= 100, fallback <= 200). Mode p, driver tristated.
"Pad" columns are fractions of VDDIO (the AIB swing) and of `iovdd` (the cell's own range).

| Cell | pad 0 | VDDIO/2 | VDDIO | iovdd/2 | iovdd | vs T-24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `IOPadInOut4mA` | 118.5 | 113.8 | 112.9 | 114.4 | 134.7 | over target, inside fallback |
| `IOPadInOut16mA` | 169.1 | 161.7 | 162.8 | 164.8 | **195.4** | over target, inside fallback (4.6 fF margin at pad = iovdd) |
| `IOPadInOut30mA` | 227.1 | 218.3 | 219.7 | 222.5 | 265.0 | **over the fallback** |
| `IOPadIn` (receive only) | 102.1 | 98.1 | 96.6 | 97.9 | 115.4 | ~target; no driver, so not a bidirectional pin |

No bidirectional PDK cell meets the 100 fF target: the smallest (4 mA) is 112.9-118.5 fF at the
AIB biases. The Liberty typical-corner pad-pin capacitance from curation (0.189 pF for 4 mA,
0.265 pF for 16 mA, `sg13g2_io_typ_1p2V_3p3V_25C.lib`) is higher than the extraction at tt/1.2 V/27 C
(about 0.11-0.13 pF and 0.16-0.18 pF). The two are not reconciled: the Liberty's contents were not
traced. What the extraction includes: the pad net of the PDK netlist (clamp devices that double as the
driver, the two `dantenna` / `dpantenna` pad diodes, secondary protection, the level-down input);
not a bond pad, not metal, not package.

**T-08, driver output resistance, ohm** (1 mA load, (iovdd - pad)/I and pad/I as `dc-static`;
T-08: target 40-80, fallback 30-120). Mode p.

| Cell | pull-up | pull-down | vs T-08 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 mA | 190.0-470.6 | 150.7-395.7 | fails both columns |
| **16 mA** | 46.1-106.8 | 37.0-88.3 | fails target (max 106.8 / 88.3 > 80), **inside fallback** |
| 30 mA | 24.5-56.4 | 19.7-46.6 | below the fallback floor (30) |

The 16 mA cell is the only member of the family inside both T-24's and T-08's fallback columns.
VOH at 1 mA is 2.86-3.58 V, VOL <= 0.088 V.

**Mode a (iovdd = VDDIO) does not drive.** With `iovdd` at the AIB level the thick-oxide output
devices are barely on: the 16 mA cell's pull-up is 177-1941 ohm and VOH at 1 mA is -0.82..1.14 V
(negative: the load wins). The PDK cell can only signal at its own 3.0-3.6 V `iovdd`. The pin
capacitance in mode a is about 11-16 % higher (16 mA: 181.2-196.8 fF at the AIB biases) and is not
a result for a working pin.

**Receiver level check** (`IOPadIn`, pad forced to 0 and VDDIO, DC only): the core-side output
is within 1.5e-5 of 0 and 1 x VDDIO at all 45 corners in both modes. This is a level check, not
a threshold, hysteresis (T-10, T-11) or timing result.

## The ruling's four statements

1. **Pin capacitance.** The cell that would be used is `sg13g2_IOPadInOut16mA`, because it is the
   only one inside both T-24's and T-08's fallback columns. Worst case 169.1 fF at the AIB swing
   (195.4 fF at pad = iovdd). T-24 target not met; T-24 fallback met.
2. **T-16 / T-08 over the channel.** T-08: above (fallback column only). **T-16: not measured**;
   see below.
3. **ESD level.** None can be cited. `libs.ref/sg13g2_io/doc/` holds a README, two HTML
   simulation notebooks and six per-corner PDFs; `grep -i esd|hbm|cdm|kv` finds nothing in the
   README or the Liberty file, and the HTML hits are inline image data. The PDFs were not opened
   (no PDF text extractor on this host), so a rating inside them is not excluded. DR-0008 found no
   ESD document in `libs.doc` either. The protection is visible in the netlist: two pad diodes
   to the I/O rails (`DCNDiode`, `DCPDiode`, 2 x 1.26 x 27.78 um `dantenna` / `dpantenna` each),
   the clamp devices that are also the driver (`Clamp_N8N8D` / `Clamp_P8N8D` at 16 mA) and the RC
   power clamp in `IOPadVdd`. The `dpantenna` model card is the one DR-0017 found to be
   "designed for the reverse direction" with `rs = 1.728e5` per unit area; by hand, 70 um^2 gives
   about 2.5 kohm, so the *model* would not carry 0.5 A through that diode either. That is a
   statement about the model, not about the silicon. Do not read the `2kv`/`4kv` names of the
   `sg13g2_esd.lib` diodes (not part of these cells) as CDM or HBM levels.
4. **Cost in the spec's terms** (if this route were taken):
   - T-22 to its 80 um fallback column; the cell leaves the 55 um class. (The ruling's cost.)
   - T-25 to its fallback column ("as the PDK's ESD documents state"): no documented level, see 3.
   - **T-06 has no fallback column.** It says "full-rail CMOS at VDDIO ... thin-oxide devices only;
     no 3.3 V devices in the data path" (DR-0004). The PDK cell drives 0..iovdd (about 3.0-3.6 V)
     through thick-oxide devices. Neither condition can be met with this cell, and the cell does
     not drive at `iovdd = VDDIO`. The ruling did not list this.
   - **T-08 to its fallback column** (16 mA cell, 46-107 / 37-88 ohm), also not listed.
   - T-24 to its fallback column (163-195 fF).
   - Rows that take VDDIO as the swing (T-09's 0.25 x VOS bound, T-26 energy per bit, T-18's
     eye bound) are not re-run; they would be restated against a 3.3 V swing. Not computed here.

## What is not shown

- **T-16 and T-08 over the declared channel with this cell.** A four-lane (then three-lane)
  channel bench like `sim/channel-eye/tb_body.inc` with the PDK cell in place of
  `aib_tx_data` / `aib_pad_esd` / `aib_rx_data` was built and run. **ngspice 47 does not produce a
  transient of the PDK cell in this setup**: the full bench ran 15 minutes at one corner with no
  measurement, and a single `IOPadInOut16mA` driving a plain 160 fF capacitor aborts with
  "Timestep too small ... trouble with node vdd#branch" (at about 3 ns) with
  `method=gear reltol=5e-3 gmin=1e-10`, and does not finish in 70 s with four other option sets
  tried. The unconverging bench is not committed; there is no T-16 number here, not even a
  failing one. Comparison point for whoever re-runs it: #251's margin is 10.873 ps nominal and
  1.331 ps at kr/kc 1.2, the +20 % C corner costing 9.5 ps. As arithmetic only (not a
  simulation): the 16 mA cell loads each end of the channel with about 160-195 fF in place of the
  baseline's ~96 fF, and 120 fF of extra channel C (the +20 % corner) costs 9.5 ps.
- No `MEAS`; no ESD rules in the DRC deck (`2AMLogic/klayout-tools#3854`); no layout of an 80 um
  cell array; no bond pad (the PDK `bondpad` is an empty placeholder), so these are the cell's
  capacitance, not the pin's in a package.
- The receiver level check is DC; no T-10 / T-11.
- Mode a is a point the PDK does not characterise; its numbers are context for why the cell
  needs its own 3.3 V `iovdd`, not a design point.
- The Liberty typical-corner capacitance was not reconciled with the extraction.

## Verdict against the ruling's test

The ruling's test is "T-24 within its fallback column AND T-16 holds with real margin". The
margin number is fixed here before the verdict: **at least 3 ps at the declared kr/kc 1.2 corner**,
i.e. about three times the bench's own <= 0.9 ps probe-vs-bench residual and no smaller than the
~3 ps crossing-definition systematic term (`sim/channel-eye/records/20261111T231422Z-t16attr`);
#251 already called 1.3 ps thin.

- T-24 within its fallback column: **yes** for the 16 mA cell (163-195 fF; 4.6 fF margin at
  pad = iovdd), no for 30 mA, yes for 4 mA but that cell fails T-08.
- T-16 with at least 3 ps of margin: **not shown** (not measured).

The test is not met, so per the ruling there is **no switch and no fallback to option (a)**; the
numbers go to the operator. Independent of T-16, the study surfaced two costs the ruling did
not list (T-06 has no fallback column and the cell cannot signal at the AIB swing; T-08 only in
its fallback column), which bear on the operator's choice.
