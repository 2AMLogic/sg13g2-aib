# sim/esd-transient

The T-25 `SIM` leg (issue #196, operator ruling q210, DR-0030): one 0.5 A CDM-class
triangular current pulse forced into `PAD` of the committed `aib_pad_esd` netlist
(`XDp1 = diodevdd_2kv`), both polarities, VDDIO powered (1.20 V) and unpowered (0 V, the
discharge case), at the single nominal corner (`mos_tt`, 1.20 V, 27 °C; T-25's grid column
is `n/a`). **Simulated**; `MEAS` is not planned and no CDM level is claimed from a
transient.

**Status: two records; the cited one is `records/20261123T211728Z/` and it FAILS DR-0030's
pre-stated isolation bound.** Peak `|V(PADI)|` on the positive pulse is 5.13 V (powered) and
3.91 V (unpowered) against the 3.5 V bound: `XDp2` is a 2 µm antenna diode that cannot
conduct. The first record, `records/20261119T085126Z/`, ran the ESD subcircuit alone (6.36 /
5.16 V) and is kept as append-only history; review round 1 (EE-key, PR #374) found that
bench omitted the driver and receiver, so the second record's DUT is the whole I/O cell
(ESD cell + tristated `aib_tx_data` on PAD + `aib_rx_data` on PADI). The measurement has
been returned to the operator (DR-0030 fail branch); T-25 is not relaxed and no bound was
edited after either run. The pad-side voltage "passes" are model self-consistency numbers
(the PSP103 build has no avalanche or snapback; `XMn1` and the driver are thin-oxide
devices with BVDSS of a few volts): they are **not** a claim that the pad survives 0.5 A.

## What was run

`request.json` (generated by `gen_esd_transient.py`, run by `run_esd_transient.sh`) on
`tb_esd_transient.cir`: one `klt sim` request, one corner, **local backend** (one unit; no
fleet, no capacity wait). Pulse shape and the pass bounds below were fixed in DR-0030
§4-5 **before** the run and were not edited after it.

- Pulse: 0.5 A peak, 200 ps rise, 400 ps fall (~400 ps at half amplitude), one event per
  polarity per window; the two polarities sit 50 ns apart so one transient covers both.
- Two tran steps alter `Vddio` between 1.20 V and 0 V; the DUT is instantiated twice:
  `Xpad` with PADI floating (the real receiver-side configuration, all voltage
  measurements) and `Xpadi` with PADI on a 0 V ammeter (the protected-branch current; the
  meter pins PADI at 0 V, a stronger stress on the secondary stage, so the reported
  current is an upper bound on the real one).
- Three DC model-check steps report the conduction models the transient credits, at the
  sizes the committed netlist uses (no design copy): `mc_dio_fwd` (pad diode forward
  I-V, cathode at 1.2 V), `mc_dio_fwd_novdd` (cathode at 0 V), `mc_xmn1_neg` (XMn1's
  drain-body junction swept in the negative-pulse direction; a positive pulse
  reverse-biases it, so `XMn1` carries no positive-pulse current). PSP103 exposes no
  device-current vector, and a mirrored sense device on a pad node would be a second,
  parallel discharge path, so `XMn1`'s transient branch current is not read out
  directly; the model check plus the pad-voltage trough and recovery evidence it.

Model check (report-only): `diodevdd_2kv` carries 0.5 A at 6.39 V over its cathode rail
(powered) and 5.19 V (unpowered) — knee ≈ 1.1 V plus `I × rs` ≈ 4.1 V, consistent with
DR-0008's "3-4 V by ohmic loss alone" and DR-0018's measured forward resistance.
`XMn1`'s junction carries 0.5 A at 1.73 V. The transient's own negative trough (1.72-1.77 V)
matches the junction number. The first record's ESD-only positive pad peaks (6.385 / 5.186 V)
matched the diode numbers; with the driver attached the peaks are lower (5.158 / 3.937 V)
because the PMOS drain-well diode shares the current through `XRsp`.

## Why the positive `V(PADI)` fails

PADI sees almost the full pad excursion because nothing holds it down: `XResd`
(≈ 165 Ω at `w=2u l=1u`) only isolates *current*, and the load it feeds is nearly open — `XDp2`
is the 2 µm × 2 µm `dpantenna` antenna diode (≈ 27 kΩ series resistance from the
`diodes.lib` card, DR-0030 review round 1), sized in DR-0017 for capacitance, and `XMn2`'s drain-body junction is
reverse-biased on a positive pulse. So PADI charges through the resistor to the pad
value within the 600 ps pulse . The asymmetry confirms it: on the negative pulse `XMn2`'s junction
(a real, low-resistance path) clamps PADI to 1.0-1.8 V and the bound passes.

A passing T-25 SIM leg needs a secondary clamp that can conduct — which trades T-24
(PADI-side capacitance counts into the receiver pin) and T-16 again. That choice is the
operator's; the numbers above are the inputs.

## What is not shown

No `MEAS`; no CDM level claimed (a 0.5 A transient is not a CDM classification, and the
"2kv" library naming is not equated with CDM, DR-0008). No `LAYOUT` current-path check.
No ESD rules in the DRC deck (`2AMLogic/klayout-tools#3854`). The gate-grounded snapback
path is neither credited nor verified (PSP103 represents the forward drain-body junction;
whether this build represents avalanche sustain is not traced). Ideal stiff rails — no
bond-wire or package inductance, no floating-rail charge sharing (no PDK bond-pad model
exists, DR-0008). Single nominal corner (T-25's grid column is `n/a`). The floating-PADI
voltage measurement assumes the receiver input is high-impedance (the `aib_rx_data`
gate); a real gate leak or a receiver ESD path of its own would change it.
