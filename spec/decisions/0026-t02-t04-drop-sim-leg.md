# DR-0026: T-02 and T-04 carry no `SIM` leg and leave the analog row set

**Status**: **proposed**. Not ratified; ratification follows
[DR-0001](0001-spec-structure-and-ratification.md) and is a human merge.
**Rows**: T-02, T-04. **Supersedes**: only the bullet of
[DR-0022](0022-t02-t04-sim-legs.md) that reads "Neither row is a covered row, and
neither is silently dropped … declared gaps". **Changes no value**: no Target, Fallback
or Stretch cell of any row moves.

## Context

DR-0022 decided what the T-02 and T-04 `SIM` legs are: T-02's is a pointer to the rows
that grade the DDR timing, and T-04's is report-only by design, because AIB 1.2 §2.1.6.3
states no clk/clkb crossing requirement. It kept both rows in the declared analog row set
as gaps. That holds item 5 / analog at `unmet` / `partial_coverage` for a reason no
verification work can clear: the legs are ungradable because **no bound exists**, not
because a testbench or tool is missing (#216).

The operator ruled on 2026-11-18 (`/fleet:decide` round 20261118-r16, q109, option a)
that the two rows carry no `SIM` leg. This record writes that ruling down; the ruling
itself is not ratified until this record is.

## Decision

1. **T-02 and T-04 carry no `SIM` leg.** Their verification cells in
   `spec/target-spec.md` name `RTL` and drop the `SIM` token. The pointers to the grading
   rows and the "reported inputs / reported evidence" wording stay.
2. **Where each row is verified.**
   - **T-02**: the exchange format by `RTL`; the DDR timing is graded by T-16, T-19 and
     T-20, which carry the numbers.
   - **T-04**: the arrangement (both wires driven and received, converted to single-ended
     at the RX) by `RTL`; every consequence of a displaced crossing is graded by T-17,
     T-16, T-06, T-09 and T-18.
3. **The analog row set drops both rows.** The derivation rule in `sim/README.md` ("every
   row with a plain `SIM` leg") now yields 19 rows rather than 21 mechanically, and
   `signoff/block-manifest.json` removes T-02 and T-04 from `spec_rows."5.analog"` and
   from `spec_rows."7.analog"`, which carries them forward.
4. **The measurements stay as reported evidence.** The 180 T-02 and 225 T-04 measurements
   in `sim/clock-path/records/20261113T095936Z/` are not edited (records are
   append-only); they remain `verdict: reported`, inputs to the rows above.
5. **No number is invented.** If a future revision of the specification, or a consumer of
   this block, states a crossing requirement, T-04 regains a `SIM` leg by a new decision
   record naming the bound (DR-0001), and the recorded measurements can be graded against
   it without re-running the grid.

## What this is not

- It is **not** a relaxation made to pass: no Target, Fallback or Stretch value changes,
  and the only edit to a ratified cell is the verification token. The two rows have not
  gone missing; item 2 says where each is verified.
- It does **not** make item 5 / analog reachable as `met`. T-05 (DR-0023) is also a
  standing gap, and T-12, T-20, T-21, T-25 and T-29 remain declared. This removes two
  standing gaps and no others.
- It does **not** re-decide the item 6 digital partition. `scripts/ci/gen_item6_digital.py`
  lists T-02 (`rtl-format`) and deliberately omits T-04. T-04's `RTL` leg is therefore not
  graded under item 6 today; that is filed separately as #327 rather than settled here.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Leave both as permanent declared gaps** (DR-0022's disposition). Rejected by the
  operator: `unmet` would read as outstanding work when none exists.
- **Delete the rows from the manifest only.** Rejected: the derivation rule would stop
  being mechanical and a row could vanish without anything flagging it. Removing the
  `SIM` token from the spec cells makes the removal follow from the rule.
- **Ask `klayout-tools` for a "no gradable leg" disposition.** Not chosen by the operator;
  it would weaken the tool's rule that an inexpressible row keeps the item unmet.

## Consequences

- `signoff/block-manifest.signoff.json` is regenerated by the pinned grader
  (`signoff/KLT_VERSION`); `declared_gaps` shortens by T-02 and T-04 in both items;
  `uncovered_rows` and `failed_rows` stay `[]`; item 5 / analog stays `unmet` while the
  remaining gaps stand.
- No record under `sim/` is edited and no simulation is re-run.
